## 正文

I just built a skill called 'Explain This'. It explains anything to you, tailored to how you learn. 

It's inspired by @geoffreylitt's talk at @aiDotEngineer: 'Understanding is the New Bottleneck'. 

AI can generate infinite code and content, and the scarce thing now is a human who actually understands.

How often have you realized you read something but retained none of it? 

I built '/explain-this' to solve that.

How it works:

🧠 It interviews you first. Ten minutes on what you know deeply, how explanations land for you, and what you're aiming to become. That generates your LEARNER.md profile.

📄 Then point your agent at any paper, PDF, or blog post and say "explain this."

✍️ It writes an explainer doc straight into your notes. Obsidian, OpenKnowledge, wherever you think, anchored to what you already know, skipping what you don't need to learn again.

✅ Every explanation ends with a quiz, and it grades your answers. Mastery learning means you don't move on until you prove to yourself you understood.

🔁 Your misses become spaced-repetition cards. Say "quiz me" next week and it re-teaches what didn't stick. Over time it learns your actual weak spots from evidence.

Works in Claude Code or any agent that supports skills. 

Free and open source. 

Link to GitHub below. 👇
