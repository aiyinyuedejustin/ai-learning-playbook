## 正文

Using AI for your own Personal Journal Club

Most people use AI to summarise papers.

I dont think that its using AI to its full potential. A better approach: treat it as a personal tutor for your own Journal Club. You read an article, AI interviews and challenges you and then it writes your answers and links everything.

You do the hard work of reading and understanding the paper’s significance

AI probes, questions, and challenges your reasoning

It writes down your responses and connects them to other notes or papers  Hopefuly you get deeper understanding and better retention.

You can add spaced repetition by having it quiz you on the same paper days or weeks later.

Any AI can do the basic version (ChatGPT, Claude…).

But agentic tools like Codex and Claude Code are significantly more powerful as they link directly to your Zotero library, Obsidian vault, PubMed, etc. 

In ChatGPT you can even use Live Voice so it talks to you and you talk back. Just like discussing with an expert colleague.

This works for any area of study: papers, books, manuscripts, legal or business documents. It’s incredibly simple but very effective.

And it’s the exact opposite of letting AI do the thinking for you.

You do the thinking.  AI just handles the note-taking and linking.
