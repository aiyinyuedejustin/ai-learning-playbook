## 正文

🚨 Stop rereading your notes and hoping you remember them.

NotebookLM can turn your study materials into an active learning system using the Feynman Technique, 80/20 Rule, active recall, spaced review, concept mapping, and self-testing.

Here are 7 powerful prompts to study smarter with your own sources. 👇👇

1️⃣ Study with the Feynman Technique
Prompt:
“Using only information from my uploaded sources, help me study [topic/concept] using the Feynman Technique. First, ask me to explain the topic in my own words as if I were teaching it to someone with no prior knowledge. Then identify any gaps, inaccuracies, or unclear parts in my explanation using evidence from my sources. Ask targeted questions to help me correct and deepen my understanding, then have me explain the topic again more clearly and simply. Do not give me the complete explanation before I attempt it myself.”

### 2026-10-16T10:26:33.000Z · https://x.com/AiWithAyesha/status/2111041096361132211

2️⃣ Study with the 80/20 Rule
Prompt:
“Using only information from my uploaded sources, help me study [topic/subject] using the 80/20 Rule. Identify the small set of core concepts, key ideas, essential terms, principles, and relationships that provide the foundation for understanding most of the topic. Prioritize them from most to least important and briefly explain why each one matters. Then create a focused study checklist showing what I should learn first, what I should review next, and what lower-priority details I can study later.”

### 2026-10-16T10:26:34.000Z · https://x.com/AiWithAyesha/status/2111041101918540225

3️⃣ Use Active Recall
Prompt:
“Using only information from my uploaded sources, help me study [topic/subject] using active recall. Create [number] questions that require me to retrieve the most important concepts, facts, terms, relationships, and processes from memory. Ask me the questions one at a time without showing the answers first. After each response, evaluate it against my sources, explain what I got right or missed, and provide the correct answer with relevant source citations. Give extra practice on areas where my recall is weak.”

### 2026-10-16T10:26:35.000Z · https://x.com/AiWithAyesha/status/2111041105685057926

4️⃣ Use Spaced Review
Prompt:
“Using only information from my uploaded sources, help me study [topic/subject] using spaced review. Identify the most important concepts, facts, terms, and relationships I need to remember, then create a review schedule for [number of days/weeks] with increasingly spaced study sessions. For each session, specify what I should review and include short retrieval questions to test my memory. Give more frequent review to difficult or easily forgotten material and gradually reduce review as my recall improves.”

### 2026-10-16T10:26:36.000Z · https://x.com/AiWithAyesha/status/2111041109795426531

5️⃣ Use the Question-Answer-Explain Method
Prompt:
“Using only information from my uploaded sources, help me study [topic/subject] using the Question-Answer-Explain method. Ask me [number] questions one at a time about the most important concepts, facts, and relationships. For each question, first have me answer from memory, then ask me to explain why or how the answer is correct in my own words. Evaluate both my answer and explanation against my sources, identify anything missing or inaccurate, and provide source-based feedback before moving to the next question.”

### 2026-10-16T10:26:37.000Z · https://x.com/AiWithAyesha/status/2111041113729745025

6️⃣ Connect Ideas with Concept Mapping
Prompt:
“Using only information from my uploaded sources, help me study [topic/subject] through concept mapping. Identify the most important concepts, terms, processes, and ideas and show how they are connected. Organize them into a clear hierarchy with the main topic at the center and related concepts branching from it. Label the relationships between concepts with short connecting phrases, and include cross-connections where relevant. Do not add relationships that are not supported by my sources.”

### 2026-10-16T10:26:38.000Z · https://x.com/AiWithAyesha/status/2111041118410362917

7️⃣ Test Your Understanding
Prompt:
“Using only information from my uploaded sources, test my understanding of [topic/subject]. Create [number] questions covering the most important concepts, facts, processes, and relationships, and ask them one at a time without showing the answers first. After each response, evaluate my answer against the sources, explain what I understood correctly and what I missed or misunderstood, and provide source-based feedback. At the end, identify my strongest and weakest areas and recommend what I should review next.”
