## 正文

Here are the top learning-related Claude Skills on GitHub, organized by the function they serve in a learning loop. You can install the ones that match your needs.

---

📥 Input — For Getting Knowledge In

expert-mentor
Turns Claude into a subject tutor for any field. It remembers what you have mastered across sessions and generates spaced-repetition flashcards. The core is a system prompt grounded in retrieval practice, spacing, and scaffolding.

teach
This is the current favorite with over 600k installs. It treats your working directory as a “teaching workspace.” You write a MISSION.md, and it produces self-contained HTML lessons, reference cheatsheets, and learning records. It explicitly distinguishes between fluency and long-term storage strength.

structured-learning-skill
A Chinese-native “structured learning” private tutor. It has a 3-step concise mode for exam cramming and a 7-step detailed mode for systematic study. It automatically downgrades based on your environment and remembers where you got questions wrong last time.

education-agent-skills
A comprehensive set of 165 evidence-backed skills covering 20 domains like pedagogy, learning science, curriculum design, and assessment. It is best for teachers and curriculum designers, but it includes a student-facing domain as well.

---

📤 Output — For Forcing Yourself to Think

sensei (3LivesLeft)
A coaching skill for engineers. It defaults to Socratic questioning and only switches to direct teaching when you are genuinely stuck. It maintains a learning profile that tracks your style, strengths, and recurring blind spots. Its philosophy: success is measured by you needing Claude less, not more.

senior-mentor
This skill runs four modes, including one that fires right after you commit code. Instead of asking “do you know this concept,” it asks “if you delete X here, what breaks?” and records the result as consistent, hit-but-wrong, or didn’t-know in a ledger.

---

✅ Verification — For Testing What You Actually Know

feynman-test (@leeat)
A Feynman technique testing skill. Claude plays a curious beginner and fires 7 types of questions (definition, motivation, analogy, boundary, relation, counterexample, playing dumb) to expose gaps. It scores you on 5 dimensions and generates a radar chart plus a blind-spot review plan.

feynman-technique (evanshlee)
A stricter version. The AI points out exactly one gap per turn, quoting your own words, and requires you to explain the point back in your own words after any scaffold. It includes a fluency illusion warning and a live companion dashboard.

smart-learn
Based on a five-step Feynman pipeline: concept map, Feynman explanation, self-test, association, and summary. It has zero core dependencies and syncs to Word plus a Mermaid mindmap in real time.

---

🧭 How to Choose

If you are starting from zero: install teach or expert-mentor first.

If you want to stop relying on Claude: add sensei to force yourself to reason.

If you think you already understand something: use feynman-test to find out if you actually do.

Most of these are single SKILL.md files you drop into ~/.claude/skills/. They are free and open source, but treat third-party skills as executable content — review them before installation.
