## 正文

The most important factor in whether or not you'll actually learn something is just whether you have the motivation.

Do you have friends who are also interested in this stuff? Do you have a project to work on? Are you trying to break into a new field?

Good explanations and technologies can help make the process smoother if you're already motivated. But they can't substitute for it.
