## 正文

AI might be making us worse at learning

A 2025 randomized study of 120 undergraduate students compared ChatGPT-assisted learning with traditional learning.

45 days later:

ChatGPT group: 57.5%
Traditional group: 68.5%

> The interesting part isn't that one group scored higher

> It's what this might tell us about how we're using AI.

> If AI summarizes the material, answers the questions, and solves the difficult parts for us, we may be removing some of the mental effort that helps information stick

> Maybe the problem isn't AI

> Maybe it's letting AI do the thinking for us

The real question isn't:

“Should we use AI?”

It's:

“What should we let AI do?”
