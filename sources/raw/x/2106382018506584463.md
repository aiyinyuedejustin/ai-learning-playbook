## 正文

I kept asking @claudeai code to explain things like MVCC, then forgot them a week later.

 So I built maxlearn, a Claude Code mod that teaches you from your chats and your interests. It puts lessons, flashcards and quizzes in the sidebar, with spaced repetition & ASD-STE100

You learn from the work you already do 🧵

How it teaches:
• Chats: skips command-only turns and keeps real concepts
• Interests: pick topics and your level. New lessons come every 24h, or when a chat touches the topic
• Don't know a term like DDL? It gets its own lesson
• Lesson first, then 2 cards join your review https://t.co/oZ1TF9mcdM

### 2026-10-03T13:53:04.000Z · https://x.com/we_chat_tech/status/2106382026647740637

It also tracks your progress:
• Strong topics and topics to improve
• Topics you use in chats each day, and time per topic
• A focus setting: daily work or interests

Pick interests and a level on first run. Don't know a term like DDL? Turn it into its own lesson. Keyboard-first.
