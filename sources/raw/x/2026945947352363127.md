prompting is by far the most important subject at any age. 

prompting is about understanding what you don't know, and understanding how to best ask the model for a response you don't understand, and perfectly balance depth and details with the higher level end goal

### 2026-02-26T09:02:28.000Z · https://x.com/gabriel1/status/2026945951282422226

i like to use the phrase "recursive gap filling". constantly inquire what you don't understand and fill the knowledge gap recursively

a great way to do this is telling a friend that you'll explain a concept from first principles, and see where you struggle
