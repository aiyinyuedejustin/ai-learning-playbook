## 正文

You have been confusing familiarity with understanding your life.

They are not the same thing.

Familiarity is the feeling you get re-reading notes you have already seen.

It feels like learning. The research calls it an illusion of knowledge.

Understanding is the ability to retrieve something from memory and apply it in a new situation. It feels uncomfortable. 

That discomfort is not evidence you are failing. 

It is evidence the learning is actually happening.

The most popular study methods produce familiarity. Active recall and spaced repetition produce understanding.

Obsidian and Claude build the knowledge graph that makes the second kind of learning twice as fast. 

Concepts connected the way your brain retains them. Claude surfacing gaps and testing retrieval automatically. 

A system that produces understanding instead of the comfortable feeling of having reviewed.

This is the complete method.
